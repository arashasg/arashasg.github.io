This research demonstrates that machine learning algorithms trained solely on normal chest X-rays can inadvertently learn to predict a patient's health insurance type. The findings highlight critical fairness concerns and potential hidden biases within medical artificial intelligence systems.

An earlier version is available as a [2025 arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11030). The citation and author order above follow the published MIDL 2026 proceedings.
